## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing, designing, and documenting a **Pharmacy Sales and Inventory Management System**.  
The system aims to automate the sales process, track medicine inventory, and provide real-time reporting to improve efficiency and accuracy.

---

##  Objectives
- Automate sales and inventory tracking.  
- Reduce errors caused by manual record-keeping.  
- Provide alerts for low stock and medicine expiry.  
- Generate detailed reports for better decision-making.  
- Ensure compliance with pharmacy regulations and data protection laws.  

---

##  Features
- **Sales Management**: Record transactions instantly.  
- **Inventory Control**: Track medicine quantities and expiry dates.  
- **Reporting**: Generate daily, monthly, and annual reports.  
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Easy to use for pharmacy staff.  
- **Security**: Data protection and compliance with regulations.  

---

##  Feasibility Study
- **Technical**: Database-driven system with barcode/RFID support.  
- **Operational**: Reduces workload and improves customer satisfaction.  
- **Economic**: Cost-effective with long-term savings.  
- **Legal**: Complies with pharmacy and data protection laws.  
- **Schedule**: Estimated timeline 3–6 months.  

---

##  Cost–Benefit Analysis
- **Software**: $2,000 – $5,000 → Saves $3,000 annually in admin errors.  
- **Hardware**: $1,500 – $3,000 → Saves $2,500 annually in staff workload.  
- **Training**: $500 – $1,000 → Increases productivity (~$1,200).  
- **Maintenance**: $800 – $1,200 → Saves $2,000 annually from downtime.  
- **Administration**: $1,200 annually → Eliminated with paperless system.  
- **User Experience**: Faster service, improved satisfaction.  

**Conclusion**: The system is economically feasible with a payback period within 1 year.
